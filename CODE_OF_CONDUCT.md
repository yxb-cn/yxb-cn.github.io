# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project discussions should be welcoming, respectful, and focused on improving
the software. Good participation includes:

- giving clear, constructive feedback;
- respecting different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- assuming good intent while addressing technical disagreements directly;
- protecting other people's privacy and unpublished work.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this standard
and may limit participation when necessary. Reports should include only the
information needed to review the conduct concern and should not repeat harmful
content more widely.

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces
managed by this project.
